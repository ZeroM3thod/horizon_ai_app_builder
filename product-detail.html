Crafted from 14 Whole Spices

Our Garam Masala Blend is the result of decades of culinary research and farmer partnerships. We source each of the 14 whole spices from their origin states — green cardamom from Kerala, cloves from Zanzibar-via-Bengal, cumin from Rajasthan — and stone-grind them in small batches for maximum freshness and potency.

Unlike mass-produced blends that use stale pre-ground spices, Shuddhota Co. stone-grinds every batch fresh. The result? A deeper, more complex flavour profile with visible spice particles — not a fine, lifeless powder.

Curries & Gravies

Add ½–1 tsp per serving of curry, dal, or vegetable gravy. Best added in the last 10 minutes of cooking to preserve fragrance. Bloom in hot oil or ghee for even deeper flavour.

timer Add with 10 min to go
rice_bowl

Biryani & Pulao

Use 1–2 tsp per kg of rice. Layer into the dum pot when adding fried onion and saffron water. Also works beautifully in white sauce-based rice dishes for fusion flavour.

restaurant 1–2 tsp per kg rice
skillet

Meats & Marinades

Rub 1 tsp into chicken, mutton, or fish with yoghurt, lemon, and salt. Marinate overnight for kebabs and tandoor-style dishes. Also excellent in kofta and seekh kabab mix.

bedtime Marinate overnight
lightbulb
Pro Tip

For the best flavour, bloom 1 tsp of garam masala in 2 tbsp of hot ghee or oil before adding other ingredients. This "tadka" technique releases fat-soluble aromatics that make the whole dish sing. Store your pouch away from heat and light for maximum shelf life — a cool, dark pantry is ideal.
