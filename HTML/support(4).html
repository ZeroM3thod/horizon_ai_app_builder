Frequently Asked Questions

Once your order is placed, you'll receive a confirmation email with your order ID within a few minutes. You can also check the "Orders" tab in your profile. If you don't see a confirmation within 30 minutes, check your spam folder or contact us.

You can cancel or modify your order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order enters our packing process and can no longer be changed. To cancel, visit your Orders page and click "Cancel Order," or contact our support team immediately.

Standard delivery within Dhaka takes 1–2 business days. Deliveries to other districts take 3–5 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM on weekdays are dispatched the same day. Express delivery (same-day in Dhaka) is available for an additional fee.

Yes! Orders above ৳ 999 qualify for free standard delivery anywhere in Bangladesh. Gold and Platinum loyalty members receive free shipping on all orders regardless of amount.

Unopened products in their original sealed packaging are eligible for return within 7 days. Due to food safety regulations, opened spice packets or dry foods cannot be returned unless they are defective or damaged on arrival.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method (bKash, Nagad, card, or COD cash return) within 3–5 business days of us receiving your return. You can also opt for store credit, which is processed instantly.

We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, all major debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and Cash on Delivery (COD) across Bangladesh. EMI options are available on orders above ৳ 5,000 with partner banks.

You earn 10 points per ৳ 100 spent. Points can be redeemed for discounts (100 points = ৳ 10 off). Reaching 3,000 points upgrades you to Platinum tier with exclusive benefits including free shipping and early access to deals.
